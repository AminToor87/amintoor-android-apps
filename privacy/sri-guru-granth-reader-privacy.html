Sri Guru Granth Sahib Ji Reader — Privacy Policy

Developer: Aminder Singh (AminToor) · Package: org.cue.granthreader

This app provides Sri Guru Granth Sahib Ji and Bani text bundled with the app for offline reading.

Reading data and settings

The scripture text is packaged with the app and can be read offline. Reading preferences, including font size, scroll speed, theme, and color palette, are saved in private app storage on your device. The app has no user account, developer-operated service, or reading-data sync feature. Its ad-request code does not send scripture text, the selected Ang or Bani, or saved reading preferences to Google as ad-targeting inputs. Preferences are removed when app data is cleared or the app is uninstalled.

Consent and advertising

The app uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) SDK to refresh consent information at app launch and to show a consent form when UMP says one is required and an applicable message is available from the AdMob configuration. The app initializes Google Mobile Ads and requests its small banner ads on the home and reading screens only when UMP's canRequestAds() check allows ad requests. UMP may allow requests based on a valid choice or status saved from an earlier session. If UMP does not allow ad requests, the app hides or removes its banner views and does not load an ad. See Google's UMP for Android guidance.

A Privacy control is available on the home and reading screens when UMP reports that a privacy-options entry point is required; it opens Google's privacy-options form. The form and available choices depend on the user's region and the messages configured by the developer in AdMob.

Google's published Google Mobile Ads SDK data disclosure describes automatic collection and sharing of data such as an IP address, product interactions (for example, app launches, taps, and video views), diagnostics, and device or account identifiers (including the Android advertising ID and app set ID where applicable) for advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention. Collection and use can depend on SDK version, device settings, consent choices, and how the services are used. Google states that collected data is encrypted in transit. This app uses Google Mobile Ads SDK 24.9.0; Google's disclosure page describes the latest SDK version, so confirm the disclosure for the exact SDK version shipped. The app does not pass scripture text or reading preferences to Google for ad targeting through its own ad-request code.

Google handles information processed by its services under its own terms. See the Google Privacy Policy.

Network and permissions

The app includes Internet and network-state access for Google consent and advertising services. The release also includes ad-identifier permissions supplied with the advertising SDK. These permissions do not create a user account or reading-data sync feature. The app does not request microphone, camera, contacts, or location access for the reading features.

Children and audience settings

The developer must select the app's target audience, age rating, and child-directed advertising settings accurately in Google Play and AdMob; this policy makes no declaration that the app is or is not directed to children.
