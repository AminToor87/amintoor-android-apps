3D File Viewer — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 1 November 2026 · Developer: Aminder Singh (AminToor) · Package: com.amintoor.viewer3d · Version 1.2.0

3D File Viewer opens and explores 3D models (STL, OBJ, PLY, 3MF, GLB, glTF) on your phone.

Summary

  • Models you open are rendered locally on your device.
  • The app has no accounts, no analytics, no ads, and no servers. We do not receive, store, or share your files.
  • File access is limited to files you explicitly choose via Android's file picker. Recent-files, favorites, and settings are kept on-device.

Permissions

The release declares no sensitive runtime permissions (no microphone, camera, contacts, or location). Sharing a model uses Android's share sheet and only acts on the file you choose.

Data we collect

We collect nothing. Your 3D models and on-device lists (recents, favorites) stay on your device.

Data sharing

No data or files are shared with us or third parties, because nothing leaves your device through this app.

Children

General-purpose 3D utility with no chat or user content sharing.
