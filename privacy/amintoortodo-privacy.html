AminToor To-Do — Privacy Policy

Developer: Aminder Singh (AminToor) · Package: com.amintoortodo.app

AminToor To-Do lets you create and manage tasks on your phone. This policy explains what stays on your device and what information may be handled when the app displays ads.

At a glance

  • Your tasks, notes, due dates and priorities are stored in the app on your device. The app does not send the contents of your tasks to Google for advertising.
  • The app can display a banner ad provided through Google Mobile Ads (AdMob). An Internet connection is used to request and display ads.
  • When ads are requested or used, Google Mobile Ads may process information such as your device’s IP address, ad and app interactions, diagnostics, and device or account identifiers. An IP address can indicate an approximate area. The information handled may depend on the SDK version, your settings and how you use the ad.
  • The app uses Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP) to refresh consent information and, where required, show a consent or privacy-options form. The app’s ad requests are subject to the consent status and ad settings in use.

Microphone and voice entry

The app asks for microphone permission only if you tap the microphone button to enter a task by voice. Voice recognition is handled by the speech-recognition service available on your Android device. Whether recognition happens on your device or uses a network service depends on your device and recognizer settings. The app does not save audio recordings or voice transcripts. It saves only the task information you choose to keep.

Information handled for ads

The Google Mobile Ads SDK may process information such as your device’s IP address; activity related to the app and ads, such as app launches and ad taps; diagnostic information about app or SDK performance; and device or account identifiers, which may include the Android advertising ID or app set ID where available. Google describes using such information to provide and measure ads, understand performance and help prevent fraud. The information and its use may vary with the SDK version, device settings, consent choices and ad interaction.

Google says information sent by the SDK is encrypted in transit using TLS. Google handles information under its own privacy terms: Google Privacy Policy. You can reset or delete the Android advertising ID through supported Android settings.

The app does not send your task titles, notes, recordings or voice transcripts to Google for ad targeting. The app does not have a user account or a server for task data. Google may process ad-related information when an ad is requested or used.

Consent and ad choices

The app uses Google’s UMP SDK to refresh consent information and show a consent message when one is required by the settings and rules that apply to you. If the configured message requires a privacy-options control, the app provides an “Ad privacy options” control that opens Google’s privacy form. The choices available, and what they mean for ads, depend on your location, the message and the app’s ad settings.

Permissions

  • RECORD_AUDIO: requested only when you choose voice entry by tapping the microphone button.
  • INTERNET and ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: used to request and display ads and check whether a network connection is available.

Information stored on your device

Tasks, due dates and notes are stored in the app on your device. Uninstalling the app removes its locally stored data.

Your task titles, notes, due dates, priorities and completion status are stored in the app’s private storage on your device. The app has no user account or developer-operated service for task data. Updating the app with the same package and signing key normally keeps its local data under Android’s update process.

Android backup is enabled for this app. Android’s backup or device-to-device transfer features may include locally stored task information when supported and enabled on your device. Whether and when a backup is created or restored depends on your Android version, device, available backup or transfer service, account availability and the relevant device and service settings. The app cannot guarantee that a backup will be created or restored. Backup and transfer copies are handled by Android and the backup or transfer service for your device; they are not received by the app developer.

You can review or change backup settings using the controls provided by Android or your device manufacturer; their location and available options vary. Uninstalling normally removes the app’s on-device data, but may not remove a separate backup copy. Whether a backup copy remains or is later restored depends on Android and the backup service’s settings and policies.

Children

AminToor To-Do is a general-purpose utility and is not designed specifically for children. Ad delivery and consent handling depend on Google’s policies, the app’s audience settings and the rules that apply in your region.
