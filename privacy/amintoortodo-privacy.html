AminToor To-Do — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 1 November 2026 · Developer: Aminder Singh (AminToor) · Package: com.amintoortodo.app · Version 1.1.0

AminToor To-Do is a lightweight task manager that works on your phone. This policy describes what the app does with your information.

Summary

  • Your tasks and notes are stored only on your device (Android SharedPreferences).
  • The app has no accounts, no analytics, no ads, and no servers. We do not receive, store, or share your data.
  • Microphone is used only when you tap the microphone button to add a task by voice.

Permissions

RECORD_AUDIO (microphone): Requested only after you tap the mic button. Voice recognition uses the Android speech-recognition service on your device, with Hindi (hi-IN) preferred. Whether recognition runs on-device or via your device's recognizer/network depends on your device settings and installed recognizer. The app does not save audio recordings or voice transcripts — it saves only the parsed task fields (title, date, priority, note) to your on-device task list.

The app declares no Internet permission and does not upload files.

Data we collect

We collect nothing. Tasks, due dates, and notes you enter stay on your device. Uninstalling the app deletes them. Updating the app with the same package and signing key preserves them via Android's normal update flow.

Data sharing

No data is shared with us or third parties, because no data leaves your device through this app.

Children

The app is a general-purpose utility with no chat, no user content sharing, and no targeted content.
