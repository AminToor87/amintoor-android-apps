AminToor Converter — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 1 November 2026 · Developer: Aminder Singh (AminToor) · Package: com.amintoor.converter · Version 1.1.0

AminToor Converter is a document toolkit (merge/split PDFs, PPTX to PDF, images to PDF) that processes files on your phone.

Summary

  • Files you open, merge, split, or convert are processed locally on your device.
  • The app has no accounts, no analytics, no ads, and no servers. We do not receive, store, or share your files.
  • File access uses Android's Storage Access Framework: the app sees only files you explicitly choose.

Permissions

The release declares no sensitive runtime permissions (no microphone, camera, contacts, or location). When you open or save a document, Android shows its file picker; grant access only to the files/folders you want to process. Converted files (e.g. Edited.pdf, AminToor_Images.pdf) are written to the location you choose.

Data we collect

We collect nothing. Your PDFs, images, and converted outputs stay on your device under your control.

Data sharing

No data or files are shared with us or third parties, because nothing leaves your device through this app.

Children

General-purpose document utility with no chat or user content sharing.
