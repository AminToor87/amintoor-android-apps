AminToor Converter — Privacy Policy

Developer: Aminder Singh (AminToor) · Package: com.amintoor.converter

AminToor Converter lets you open and convert supported documents on your device. This policy explains how the app handles selected files and what information may be processed when it displays ads.

At a glance

  • Selected PDFs, images, presentations and converted files are processed on your device. The app does not send file contents to Google for advertising.
  • The app can display a banner ad provided through Google Mobile Ads (AdMob). An Internet connection is used to request and display ads.
  • When ads are requested or used, Google Mobile Ads may process information such as your device’s IP address, ad and app interactions, diagnostics, and device or account identifiers. An IP address can indicate an approximate area. The information handled may depend on the SDK version, your settings and how you use the ad.
  • The app uses Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP) to refresh consent information and, where required, show a consent or privacy-options form. The app’s ad requests are subject to the consent status and ad settings in use.

Files and permissions

The app uses Android’s Storage Access Framework. Android’s file picker lets you choose the files or folders the app can open, and where converted files can be saved. The app has no user account. Selected files and converted output are processed locally and are not sent to Google for advertising.

The app uses INTERNET and ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE to request and display ads and check whether a network connection is available. It does not request runtime permissions for the microphone, camera, contacts or location.

Information handled for ads

The Google Mobile Ads SDK may process information such as your device’s IP address; activity related to the app and ads, such as app launches and ad taps; diagnostic information about app or SDK performance; and device or account identifiers, which may include the Android advertising ID or app set ID where available. Google describes using such information to provide and measure ads, understand performance and help prevent fraud. The information and its use may vary with the SDK version, device settings, consent choices and ad interaction.

Google says information sent by the SDK is encrypted in transit using TLS. Google handles information under its own privacy terms: Google Privacy Policy. You can reset or delete the Android advertising ID through supported Android settings.

The app does not send the contents of your selected PDFs, images, presentations or converted files to Google for ad targeting. Google may process ad-related information when an ad is requested or used.

Consent and ad choices

The app uses Google’s UMP SDK to refresh consent information and show a consent message when one is required by the settings and rules that apply to you. If the configured message requires a privacy-options control, the app provides an “Ad privacy options” control that opens Google’s privacy form. The choices available, and what they mean for ads, depend on your location, the message and the app’s ad settings.

Children

AminToor Converter is a general-purpose document utility and is not designed specifically for children. Ad delivery and consent handling depend on Google’s policies, the app’s audience settings and the rules that apply in your region.
